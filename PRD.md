# PRD: Chef Sam - Unified Experience

## Core Concept
A single-identity website. The focus is 100% on the Cameroonian experience and Chef Sam's 10-year mastery (desde 2016). 

## Navigation & Structure
- **Home:** One main landing page for the brand.
- **Unidades Section:** A clear area on the homepage (and footer) displaying both Tatuapé and Pinheiros addresses side-by-side.
- **WhatsApp:** A single point of contact or a simple "Choose your unit" popup upon clicking the contact button.

## Aesthetic
- Unified Afro-Brazilian luxury.
- Terracotta, Gold, and Charcoal throughout.
- Constant use of Ndop patterns.
